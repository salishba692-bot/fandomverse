Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to our most common community questions:

Is FandomVerse 100% free to use?
Yes! FandomVerse is completely free for all fans to browse, create accounts, participate in discussion threads, vote on theories, and connect with communities.
How do I submit a new Fandom to be added?
Simply fill out the contact form on this page with the subject "New Fandom Suggestion" or email marinacaipher09@gmail.com. We regularly add realms requested by our community.
Can I share my original artwork or cosplay?
Absolutely! When creating a new thread, select "Cosplay" or "Fan Art Showcase" as your post type. Original creators are celebrated here.
How do I become a community moderator?
Active, helpful, and respectful community members are frequently reached out to by Alishba Shahid to assist with realm moderation.
